# Recovery Proof Kit — landing page draft

## Headline

Your backup says “success.” Can your application actually recover?

## Subheadline

Recovery Proof Kit helps small SaaS teams, agencies, and MSPs verify backup freshness and integrity, document recovery gaps, and plan isolated restore tests—without uploading backup data.

## What it checks

- Is the expected backup present?
- Is it fresh enough for your RPO?
- Is it large enough to be meaningful?
- Does its digest match the expected artifact?
- Is an offsite copy documented?
- Is immutability documented?
- Has the application actually been restored recently?

## What it does not do

It does not replace your backup provider, modify production, or claim that a checksum alone proves recoverability.
