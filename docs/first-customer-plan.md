# First-customer plan

## Target list

Build a list of 30 prospects:

- 10 MSPs serving small businesses;
- 10 software agencies hosting customer applications;
- 10 small SaaS companies with public status pages or engineering blogs.

## Outreach offer

Offer a free, read-only recovery review using their existing backup process. Do not request production credentials or backup data. Ask them to run the kit locally and share only the generated report.

Suggested message:

> I’m testing a small recovery-evidence kit for teams that already have backups but have not recently proved they can restore. It runs locally, does not upload backup data, and checks freshness, integrity, and the recovery plan. I’m looking for five operators to try it against a non-production environment. Would a 20-minute walkthrough be useful?

## Discovery questions

1. What system performs your backups?
2. What exactly counts as a successful backup today?
3. When did you last restore a real application into an isolated environment?
4. Who receives failures, and how are they tracked?
5. What would a one-hour outage cost?
6. Do customers or insurers ask for recovery evidence?
7. Which systems should the kit support first?

## Paid conversion

After a successful self-serve test, offer the $19 Beta Edition. Reserve the $79 guided pilot for customers who specifically want implementation help. Ask for payment before building an integration they requested. A request without payment is a feature suggestion, not validation.
